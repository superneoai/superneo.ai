Privacy

RETURN TO SUPERNEO

Analytics on superneo.ai are optional. Nothing is sent to PostHog until you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not change the experience.

Controller

Superneo operates superneo.ai and determines why the information described here is processed. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to hello@superneo.ai.

Local and necessary storage

The site may remember your soundtrack volume locally. After you make an analytics choice, it stores an sn_consent record containing the choice, consent revision, a random consent identifier and timestamps. This prevents the site from repeatedly asking. The record expires after 180 days unless the policy changes sooner.

Optional analytics

If allowed, the site sends a limited event set to PostHog:

  • page path, referring hostname and intentionally supplied campaign tags;
  • scene readiness and stage-completion milestones;
  • bounded signal interactions, soundtrack state and a bucketed volume value;
  • outbound destination category; and
  • Core Web Vitals and a coarse device class.

Events use a random browser identifier. Superneo does not send names, email addresses, typed text, exact pointer paths or full URLs containing arbitrary query strings. PostHog autocapture, personal profiles, session replay, heatmaps, surveys, advertising and automatic error capture are disabled.

Purpose and legal basis

The purpose is to understand whether the experience loads, performs and reaches its stages. The legal basis for optional analytics is consent. You can refuse or withdraw without losing access.

Processor, location and transfers

PostHog, Inc. processes optional analytics for Superneo using PostHog Cloud US. Primary cloud storage is in the United States. PostHog's Data Processing Agreement incorporates the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses for covered transfers. PostHog's current subprocessors are listed at posthog.com/subprocessors.

Retention and security

The browser analytics identifier is limited to 180 days. Analytics events are retained for no more than 12 months, then deleted or aggregated. IP capture must remain disabled in the PostHog organization and project. Network and storage access are limited to what is necessary to provide the service.

Your choices and rights

Use the PRIVACY control in the site footer to change or withdraw your analytics choice. Withdrawal stops new collection and removes PostHog browser storage. Global Privacy Control is treated as a decline. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of information associated with your consent or analytics identifier. Contact hello@superneo.ai.

No sale or advertising

Superneo does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The analytics described here are not used to build advertising profiles.
